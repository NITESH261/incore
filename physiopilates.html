What is Physio Pilates?

Physio Pilates is a specialised rehabilitation approach that combines detailed physiotherapy assessment with targeted Pilates-based movement therapy. It focuses on restoring muscle balance, improving posture and strengthening deep stabilising muscles that support the spine and joints.

Sessions are designed to improve flexibility, coordination and breathing control while supporting long-term injury prevention. The structured approach helps patients regain confidence in movement and return to daily activities with improved strength and stability.

At InCore Physiotherapy, Physio Pilates is integrated into personalised treatment plans to support rehabilitation after injury, manage chronic pain conditions and enhance overall functional performance.

Who Can Benefit from Physio Pilates?

Physio Pilates is a safe and effective approach for people recovering from injury, managing chronic pain or improving movement control through personalised rehabilitation programmes.

For Injury Recovery

  • Supports muscle and ligament healing
  • Post-operative rehabilitation guidance
  • Improves flexibility and movement confidence
  • Restores functional strength safely

For Back & Joint Conditions

  • Reduces chronic back pain symptoms
  • Improves posture and spinal alignment
  • Enhances joint stability and mobility
  • Strengthens deep core muscles

For Neurological & Chronic Conditions

  • Supports neurological movement recovery
  • Improves coordination and balance
  • Helps manage arthritis and fibromyalgia
  • Enhances strength and functional control
