What is Guided Meditation?

Guided meditation is a structured relaxation practice that helps calm the mind, regulate breathing and improve emotional stability. It supports mental clarity, reduces anxiety and enhances focus.

At InCore Physiotherapy, meditation sessions are designed to complement physical rehabilitation by improving nervous system balance and reducing stress-related muscle tension.

These sessions are suitable for individuals experiencing anxiety, sleep disturbances, chronic pain or emotional fatigue.

Benefits of Meditation

Stress Reduction

Helps calm the nervous system and reduce daily stress levels.

Improved Sleep

Encourages deeper relaxation and better sleep quality.

Mental Clarity

Enhances focus, awareness and emotional control.

Pain Support

Supports chronic pain management through relaxation techniques.
