Welcome to InCore Physiotherapy in Henley-on-Thames

At InCore, we're dedicated to providing exceptional physiotherapy services. Whether it's back pain, joint discomfort or muscle tension from poor posture, our experienced physiotherapists are here to help.

We take a holistic approach, identifying and addressing the root causes of your discomfort for lasting results.

Healing from the core is the essence of our practice. We combine expert physiotherapy with core-strengthening methods like Pilates and Physio Pilates, alongside holistic wellness services including Acupuncture, Meditation and Pranic Healing.

This powerful integration delivers comprehensive, transformative care for your physical health and overall wellbeing.

Meet Your Expert Physiotherapist

Ashwini Auti

Bachelor of Physiotherapy (B.P.Th.)

Master of Science (MSc) in Neuro Physiotherapy from Oxford Brookes University

Postgraduate Certificate in Medical Education

Ashwini Auti, the founder of InCore Physiotherapy in Henley-on-Thames, brings over 15 years of extensive experience as a Physiotherapist, Acupuncturist and expert Pilates instructor.

As a registered professional with the MHCPC, MCSP and MAACP and a Fellow of the Higher Education Academy, Ashwini is committed to delivering expert care using the latest evidence-based approaches.

Her unique approach, built on “healing from the core,” looks beyond symptoms to identify root causes across physical, emotional and spiritual health.

She creates personalised treatment plans combining physiotherapy, Pilates, PhysioPilates, acupuncture, meditation and Pranic Healing for long-term recovery and lasting wellbeing.
