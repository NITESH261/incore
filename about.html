Our Approach to Healing

At InCore Physiotherapy, we believe true recovery begins by understanding the root cause of discomfort — not just treating symptoms. Every person’s body is unique, which is why our treatment approach focuses on identifying the underlying source of pain and restoring natural movement patterns.

We combine evidence-based physiotherapy with holistic therapies including Clinical Pilates, Acupuncture, Meditation and Breathwork to support complete recovery across body, mind and awareness. This integrated approach helps reduce pain, improve mobility and prevent future injury.

Rather than offering short-term relief alone, our goal is to create lasting change by strengthening the body from its foundation — the core. Through personalised treatment plans, guided rehabilitation and movement education, we empower patients to take control of their health journey.

Our philosophy “Healing from the Core” reflects our commitment to supporting long-term wellbeing, resilience and balance in everyday life.

Our Philosophy

At InCore Physiotherapy, we believe healing should address the root cause of discomfort — not just symptoms. Our integrated approach supports long-term wellbeing across physical, emotional and mental health.

Our Vision

To empower individuals to live balanced, pain-free lives by restoring movement, strength and confidence through holistic physiotherapy care.

Our Mission

To provide personalised, evidence-based treatment plans that combine physiotherapy, Pilates, acupuncture and mindfulness practices for lasting recovery.
