What is Pilates?

Pilates is a specialised exercise method that uses slow, controlled movements to strengthen deep stabilising muscles that support posture, protect joints and improve flexibility.

It focuses strongly on core strength, spinal alignment and breathing control, helping reduce pain, prevent injuries and improve balance, coordination and body awareness.

Regular Pilates sessions support long-term musculoskeletal health and are particularly beneficial for people recovering from injuries, experiencing chronic pain or aiming to improve posture and mobility.

At InCore Physiotherapy, Pilates is integrated into rehabilitation programmes to enhance recovery outcomes and promote lasting physical wellbeing.

We offer various types of Pilates classes

Mat Pilates

Mat Pilates is a structured form of therapeutic exercise performed on a supportive floor mat that focuses on strengthening the deep stabilising muscles of the abdomen, back and pelvis. It helps improve posture, flexibility, breathing coordination and balance while reducing muscle tension and preventing injury.

This method is especially beneficial for individuals recovering from musculoskeletal pain, improving spinal alignment or building core strength for long-term physical stability and mobility.

Fusion Pilates – Yoga

Fusion Pilates combines the strength-building principles of clinical Pilates with the flexibility and relaxation techniques of Yoga. This integrated approach enhances breathing control, movement awareness and joint mobility while promoting muscular balance throughout the body.

It is particularly effective for stress relief, improving coordination and supporting overall wellbeing, making it ideal for individuals seeking both rehabilitation support and functional fitness improvement.
